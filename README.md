## 💡 Key Insights
- **Total Volume & Sales:** Analyzed 32K tickets generating 742K in total revenue.
- **Peak Activity:** Morning is the most active period (10.0K tickets), with 17:00 being the single busiest hour.
- **On-Time Performance:** 86.82% of journeys arrived on time, while weather and technical issues were the leading causes of the 7% delays.
- **Booking Preferences:** Advance tickets accounted for the highest sales (309K), and online bookings outpaced station purchases (19K vs. 13K).
